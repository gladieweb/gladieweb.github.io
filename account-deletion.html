Account and data deletion

Request deletion of your Skeleton Warfare account

Skeleton Warfare is developed by Indie Soul Games. Use this page to request deletion of your game account and the data associated with it.

Request deletion on Instagram Request deletion by email

How to request deletion

  1. Contact Indie Soul Games through Instagram or email using the links on this page.
  2. State that you want to delete your Skeleton Warfare account and associated data.
  3. Include the account identifier you use in the game, such as your player ID, login email, or linked Google Play account, so we can locate your account.
  4. We may ask for additional information to verify ownership before deleting the account.

Data deleted

We will delete or anonymize account data, game progress, gameplay records connected to your account, support messages linked to the request, and identifiers used to connect your account to the game.

Data retained

Some records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, payment records, or dispute handling. These records are kept only as long as necessary for those purposes, and generally no longer than 180 days unless a longer period is required by law.
