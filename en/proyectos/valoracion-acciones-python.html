Stock valuation with Python

Stock lookup by ticker, intrinsic value estimation, and the percentage of undervaluation or overvaluation relative to market price.

The context

What was needed

Automating part of value investing research through a component that can be integrated into a website.

My contribution

What I developed

I developed a Python script that accepts one ticker, such as AAPL, or a list of tickers. It retrieves information through APIs such as Yahoo Finance, estimates intrinsic value, fetches the market price, and shows the percentage of undervaluation or overvaluation.

  • Input of one or multiple stock tickers.
  • Retrieval of information and prices through APIs such as Yahoo Finance.
  • Estimated intrinsic value calculation and percentage comparison with the quoted price.
  • Status: implemented Python script, intended as a microservice component. The website has not yet been developed.
